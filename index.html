Clipper

Clipper is a personal tool that turns long-form videos into short vertical clips with captions and uploads them to its owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

It only uses videos that may be reused: videos published under the Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY) licence, and videos from creators who have given permission. Every clip credits the original creator and links to the source video.

YouTube API Services

Clipper uses YouTube API Services (the YouTube Data API videos.insert method) to upload clips to the owner's channel. By using Clipper you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Clipper is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public.
