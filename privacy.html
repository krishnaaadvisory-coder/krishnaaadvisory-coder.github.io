Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Clipper is a personal application operated by its owner for their own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It is not available to other users.

Data accessed

Clipper uses the YouTube Data API (scope: youtube.upload) and the Instagram API solely to upload videos to the owner's own accounts. It does not read, collect or store data about any other person.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's private computer and are never shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and in Instagram settings.

Clipper's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
